# Land of Memes: Whitepaper

## Vision

A shared map of internet culture where every meme owns a patch of ground, and where anyone can see at a glance what the crowd is trading, laughing at and building around.

## Mission

Give meme culture a home that is playful to explore and honest about what it knows. Facts that are confirmed are shown. Facts that are not confirmed are marked pending, never guessed.

Announce the contract address and the official channels in one place, then grow the community around them.

### Phase 3: The map

Release the first version of the land map, with meme territories that holders can explore.

### Phase 4: Expansion
